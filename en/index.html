Indoor air and water in Italy: guides in English

Casa Respira is an independent Italian guide to the air and water inside homes. Most of it is written in Italian, for Italian readers. These pages are the exception: short, sourced guides in English for people who buy, rent or invest in property in Italy and need to understand the Italian rules.

How these pages are written

Same method as the Italian site: every figure comes from an institutional source (Italian law, the National Institute of Health, regional environmental agencies) with a link and a date; no prices unless verified; corrections are declared on the page. We do not give medical advice. Where a topic has a full Italian guide, we link to it.
